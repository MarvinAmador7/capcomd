The name opens the project's menu. Switching projects is the decks' job, so this menu only holds what you can do to the open project, and only actions the daemon has today.
The top tells you what the project is. The whole goal as you wrote it, the repos it touches, and where it runs: Local or Cloud with the provider, the number of agents, and when it started.
Work with it. Message the planner (the run's messages route), Changes with the files and their counts (the run's files), Trace, and Browse the workspace.
Control it. Pause becomes Resume while paused. Stop asks first, because it cancels every agent; it is the one red item.
Fold, don't close. ⌘W folds the project back into its deck and it keeps running; that's the only kind of close a running project gets. A finished project offers Remove from the title bar instead.
Left out on purpose: rename, duplicate, archive and export. Nothing in capcomd does them, and the goal is the name.
