Expand / collapse. The arrows-out button at a pane's top right (or ⇧⌘↩) gives that pane the whole window: sidebar hidden, content in a centred 800 pt column, diffs and logs wider, the ask bar at column width. Esc or arrows-in returns to the split or sidebar it came from.

⌘-click a row opens it beside the focused pane. A split opens the next agent in tree order. × or ⌘W closes a split; with one pane left, ⌘W folds the project as approved. Splits are kept per project.

The magnifying glass in the sidebar header (or ⌘K anywhere) opens one palette for projects, agents and commands: folded, opened, with a pane expanded or in the Needs you reel. A glass, not a field: the 248 pt header already holds the lights, the sidebar button, the hand and +, and a field would need a row of its own.

Ranked by attention: Needs you first, then agents (by their own titles), projects, and commands. ↩ opens, ⌘↩ opens beside the focused pane.

Commands are only real actions of the daemon and the app (new project, message, changes, trace, pause or resume, stop, expand, split, sidebar, settings), each with its shortcut. No network search: it searches what the app already holds, so results appear as you type.

What moves

Nothing loops and nothing glows. Running rows have no spinners: their status is a word. A row moves only when its agent changes section (an ask arrives: it leaves Running and appears in Needs you, 0.2 s opacity and a 6 pt slide), and never under the pointer: the move waits until the pointer leaves the sidebar. The pill and ••• fade in over 120 ms. Reduce Motion: crossfades.

Performance

The sidebar is its own list view with fixed heights (28 pt rows and headings, 24 pt labels), fed by .projects, .run(id), .agent(id) and .approvals, never .thread. Scrolling it moves its own clip view and touches no pane. It is a fixed 248 pt while open and titles truncate, so nothing in it can resize a pane; opening and folding move layers, and the panes lay out once, at rest.
