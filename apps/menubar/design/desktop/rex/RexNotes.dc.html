Owner's decisions (2026-11-09). C is the direction: folded, its rail; opened, a Rex-style sidebar. The sidebar is organised by attention, not by machine: Needs you, Running, Finished. "User's attention should be on things that need the user, not just visual entertainment." The Needs you icon is SF hand.raised, the app's existing ask symbol ("the hand is fine"). All chrome follows macOS native components (NSPanel/NSMenu/vibrancy), never web-UI kits.

What it replaces, opened

  • Decks and crew tabs leave the title bar, and the title bar goes: each pane's header is the top line.
  • The reel's three columns become one pane plus splits.
  • Crew leaves the rail while open: the rows are the crew.
  • Folded, nothing changes: C's rail, the approved Reel, Home's asks.

Changed from the brief

  • Places are an icon on the project label, once per project: every agent of a project shares its place.
  • The project is a label, not a row subtitle: it keeps one 28 pt line per agent and full-width titles.
  • Rows show the title only; profile and ID are in the pane header.
  • Usage limits and errors sit in Needs you as decisions, not as row badges.
  • Rex's eye became the Needs you control (owner). Follow stays a mode, in ⌘K, the menus and ⇧⌘F, and folded in the approved title bar.
  • Provider badges are placeholders until the official one-colour marks are added.

The Needs you hand

  • SF hand.raised. While anything needs you: the hand in the normal icon colour, the amber count beside it (a badge on the folded rail). At zero: grey, no count.
  • Click opens the Needs you reel, as the section heading does: one column per asking agent, oldest first, each with its ask bar. Answering slides that column out (under 300 ms) and the next takes its place; with none left, "Nothing needs you." and Back (Esc). A row still opens just that agent; ⌘J steps through the asks one at a time.

Expand a pane (owner)

  • Arrows-out at a pane's top right, or ⇧⌘↩ (the approved zoom key): the pane takes the window, sidebar hidden, a centred 800 pt reading column; diffs and logs run wider; the ask bar sits at column width.
  • Esc or arrows-in returns to the split or sidebar it came from. Expansion is per pane and keeps its scroll position; switching agents from ⌘K while expanded stays expanded.

Search ⌘K (owner)

  • A magnifying glass in the sidebar header replaces Actions ⌘K (a field would need its own row at 248 pt). ⌘K works anywhere: folded, opened, a pane expanded, the Needs you reel.
  • One palette for projects, agents and commands, ranked Needs you first, then agents, projects, commands; ↩ opens, ⌘↩ opens in a split. Commands are only real daemon and app actions, with their shortcuts. No network search: it searches what the app already holds.
