+ at the right end of the title bar, or ⇧⌘N, opens a new project. The + stays held down while it's open, and the other projects fold into their stacks. Once it starts, the project takes its own tab after the others.
A new project is one column. No agent yet, so no header: just the same message box every column has. One card holds it all: the folder and branch on top, the goal at 14 pt, then a single tool row with + (folder, file or skill), Local or Cloud, the model with its effort bars, the mic, and Start. Click the mic or hold fn to speak the goal: the words land in the field as you talk, so you can fix them before Start. The blue ring shows focus; Return starts the run.
Start turns it into the planner's column. The planner's header appears above your goal, which becomes its first message; the tab takes the project's name and place, and the crew's columns fill the dashed space as the planner spawns them.
The charm is the bot who'll plan it. Lumen waits in the empty column with a sleepy face, smiles once you start typing (one change, no loop), and on Start hops up into the column's header to become the planner. Your goal is the first thing it hears.
