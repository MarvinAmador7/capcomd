Reel ruleBehaviour
OrderTree order, left to right: planner, then each lead followed by its workers, then the critic. A column never moves on its own.
WidthThree columns fit a 1440 pt window, two at 1100, one under 760. Drag a column's edge to widen it; the reel keeps that width for this agent.
Follow (the eye)When on, an ask or a failure scrolls the reel to that column in 0.35 s, spring, no bounce. Scrolling the reel yourself turns Follow off until you press it again.
Keyboard⌃← and ⌃→ move focus one column; ⌃1–9 jump to an agent. Focus moves at once; the reel only scrolls, 180 ms ease-out, when the focused column would leave the window.
TrackpadTwo-finger swipe scrolls with momentum and snaps to a column edge. Past the first or last column the reel resists with friction instead of stopping hard.
