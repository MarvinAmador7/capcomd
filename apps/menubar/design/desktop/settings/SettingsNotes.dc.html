Settings · notes and open choices

Structure

  • One window, sidebar and pane: the desk’s chrome (the 248 pt sidebar on the ground, its 28 pt rows and selection; the pane 8 pt in), 880 × 660 pt, taller by dragging. SwiftUI: a NavigationSplitView in the Settings scene, .searchable(placement: .sidebar), each page today’s grouped Form in a 624 pt pane (today’s window is 620 pt).
  • Sidebar: Search, then the capcomd status row (Rex’s “Server · Running”: the menu bar mark, Running and the version; red Not running), then General · Notifications | Interface · Shortcuts | Runs · Accounts | About. Four tabs become eight pages because capcomd’s own state, layout, notifications and shortcuts each get one page instead of sharing General.
  • Toolbar: Back and Forward in a capsule (⌘[ ⌘]) and the page’s title. Back walks pages and pushed pages alike.
  • Progressive disclosure: a place is one row on Runs; its environment, machines, safety and options are on its page. A row shows the one state that needs action (“Agent environment not built”).
  • Labels and values only: the explaining sublabels go. A fact the person needs stays as one ⓘ footnote under its card (⌃⌘S, Terminal for logins, System Settings for banners).
  • Search: every row’s label and its page, plus the places and logins the store holds; results replace the list, ↩ opens the first and rings the matching card for a second.
  • Attention: a login the default driver needs where new runs go puts the rail’s amber count on Accounts and the prominent Log In… on its row.
  • The layout setting: capcomd’s chrome is already Rex’s Above / Left choice: Reel puts the crew as tabs above the columns, Sidebar lists them at the side. The tiles pick it (the value ⌃⌘S already stores); the rows under them set the side, the sidebar’s behavior and the reel’s columns and tabs.

Daemon work

  • None for what is drawn. Every page reads today’s routes: /v1/health, /v1/service, /v1/run-options, /v1/logins, /v1/environments, /v1/sandboxes and its resources, settings and actions.
  • Left off until the daemon has it: Log Out of a login (no route), an environment build’s progress in the app (D3), Restart capcomd (no route).

Open choices (yours)

  • Chrome: the desk’s sidebar and pane (drawn), or the system’s macOS 26 sidebar with its accent-blue selection.
  • Appearance: dark only, as the desk (drawn); Settings follows the system today (GeneralLight board). Light needs light desk tokens.
  • Attention badge: the rail’s amber count (drawn) or System Settings’ red; and a dot on the desk’s Settings gear too, or not.
  • Show on hover: keep it (the most new work on Interface) or ship Always show only.
  • Shortcuts: read-only (drawn; System Settings › App Shortcuts can rebind menu items) or rebinding in the app.
  • Columns across: keep, or leave the reel’s fixed rule.

How it would ship

  • 1 · The window, sidebar, status row, search, Back / Forward, place pages, Model row. Moves only, no new settings.
  • 2 · Interface: Layout tiles, sidebar position, Follow, crew tabs, columns across; then Show on hover.
  • 3 · Notifications: usage limits and the Dock badge. Shortcuts page.
