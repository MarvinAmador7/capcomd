# CHANGELOG

- The desktop window has four layouts: **Tabs above**, **Sidebar left**, **Sidebar right** and **Tabs below**. Tabs and
  the sidebar are never shown together: with tabs, the crew is a row of tabs over (or under) the columns, and Home,
  Team, History, Settings, the Needs you hand with its count, Search (⌘K) and + sit at the tab bar's trailing end;
  with the sidebar, it is on the side you chose and the traffic lights stay at the top left. Choose one from
  View › Layout. ⌃⌘S still switches between tabs and the sidebar, and keeps the side or edge you chose for each.
- The folded icon rail is gone: its buttons are in the tab bar, with the same tooltips and shortcuts.
- View › Layout › Show Sidebar on Hover and Show Tab Bar on Hover keep the sidebar or the tab bar out of the way until
  the pointer reaches its edge of the window (it goes again when the pointer leaves). Both are always shown while
  VoiceOver is on.
- Your current choice carries over: the Reel becomes Tabs above, and the Sidebar becomes Sidebar left.
